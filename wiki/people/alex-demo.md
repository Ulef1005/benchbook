## Conventions this page demonstrates

- **`wiki/people/` is humans only.** Software personas, channels, brands and products are not
  people, however much they have names — those go to `knowledge/entities/` or a domain's own
  `entities/` per the Entity Placement Rule.
- **Person pages carry no `status:` field.** The owned/considered/retired vocabulary makes no
  sense for a person.
- **`domain: null`** — people are global, not owned by a domain.
- Domain packs may add sections here. The cooking pack, for instance, adds a food-preferences
  section used for meal planning.

## A note on privacy

Other people did not choose to be in your wiki, and they can't check what you wrote. This is
the category that most deserves restraint — see [13 — Privacy](../../docs/13-privacy.md).
