Quick notes on panel sizes. 4.2" is the sweet spot for a hallway — readable from a couple of
metres, still cheap. 7.5" is nicer to look at but roughly triples the price and needs a bigger
enclosure than you want by a door.

Partial refresh support varies by panel, not just by driver. Check before buying.
